FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How many dogs can you care for at home?

Four dogs maximum total at any one time, including boarding and daycare.

Do drop-in visits count toward that limit?

No. Drop-in visits happen at the client's home and are separate from home boarding/daycare capacity.

When does boarding count as another day?

Pick-up by 7 AM the following day keeps the stay at the overnight rate. Pick-up after 7 AM adds a full boarding day.

What do I need to bring?

Leash, food, food bowls, current vaccination proof, and a completed client contract. Treats are optional.

Is the contract signed every time?

No. It is signed once unless the agreement is updated.

What are holiday rates?

Holiday pricing is the regular rate plus $10. Holiday bookings require a 50% non-refundable deposit.
