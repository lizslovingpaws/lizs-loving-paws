About Liz

Professional experience, personal care.

Hi, I'm Liz. I believe pets deserve the same patience, kindness, and attention we'd want for our own family members.

My animal-care background includes four years as a veterinary technician, managing a puppy store where I regularly cared for 15–20 puppies during a shift, learning from local breeders, and volunteering with the Humane Society.

Liz's Loving Paws was created to give families a dependable option for pet care that feels personal rather than crowded.

My approach

  • Safety and comfort first
  • Individual attention
  • Clear communication
  • Small-scale home care
  • Loving treatment of every pet
